# AI Discovery Engine - 2 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:20 | The Problem
"Hi everyone. Millions of users browse Myntra and save products to their wishlists, but many of those products are never purchased. I wanted to understand the non-monetary reasons behind this drop-off. Why do users wishlist products but fail to convert within 30 days?"

## 0:20–0:40 | The Engine
"To answer this, I built the AI Discovery Engine. Instead of just guessing, this engine analyses unstructured, user-generated evidence—like reviews, Reddit threads, and social comments—and identifies recurring behavioural and UX barriers."

## 0:40–1:10 | The Workflow
"Here is how it works: First, it **extracts** signals from the raw text—determining if the user actually had purchase intent. Then, it **classifies** those signals into specific abandonment barriers, like 'Fit Uncertainty' or 'Product Visualization'. Next, it **clusters** recurring patterns and uses our Opportunity Scorer to **prioritise** them based on frequency and severity."

## 1:10–1:40 | Example Insight
"Let's look at an actual output from the engine. 

* **Evidence:** Users frequently say things like, 'I liked the product but wasn't sure how it would look on me' or 'I need to see reviews first.'
* **Behavioural Barrier:** The AI classified this as a *Product Confidence Gap*, specifically related to size and fit.
* **Insight:** Wishlisting represents genuine interest, but users lack enough confidence in the fit to move from consideration to purchase."

## 1:40–2:00 | PM Value
"The output isn't intended to be the final solution. What it does is give the Growth team structured, evidence-backed hypotheses. For example, our top opportunity here is to improve product visualization and fit-context information. We can now take this prioritized hypothesis and validate it through targeted experiments."
