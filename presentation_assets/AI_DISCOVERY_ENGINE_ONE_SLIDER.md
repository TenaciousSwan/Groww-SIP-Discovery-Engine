# AI Discovery Engine: Turning User Signals into Growth Opportunities
**How we identify why wishlisted products fail to convert into purchases**

---

## 1. COLLECT
**Reviews | Reddit | Social | Surveys | User Research**
Unstructured user signals are ingested in real-time.

↓

## 2. EXTRACT
**AI identifies relevant behavioural signals**
The engine isolates purchase intent, friction points, and specific triggers.

↓

## 3. CLASSIFY
**Maps signals to abandonment barriers**
Translates raw text into standard barriers (e.g., *Product Confidence*, *Fit Uncertainty*, *Occasion-Not-Yet*).

↓

## 4. CLUSTER + PRIORITIZE
**Groups recurring themes and scores opportunities**
Our opportunity scoring algorithm sizes the friction based on frequency and severity to pinpoint the biggest leaks.

↓

## 5. ACT
**Converts barriers into product & Growth hypotheses**
Equips the Growth team with data-backed, testable solutions rather than guesswork.

---

### 💡 EXAMPLE OPPORTUNITY

**USER SIGNAL**
> *"I liked it but wasn't sure how it would look on me."*

↓

**BEHAVIOURAL BARRIER**
> *Product Confidence Gap (Size/Fit Doubt)*

↓

**PM OPPORTUNITY**
> *Improve product visualization and fit-context information.*
